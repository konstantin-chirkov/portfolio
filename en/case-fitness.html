A fitness app for an influencer with an audience of 2M+ followers: workout programmes, challenges, progress trackers, a subscription and a referral programme.

  • B2C
  • Mobile
  • Web
  • FitTech
My role
Product Designer
Year
2024–2025
Reading time
2 minutes

The task

Build a digital hub for the influencer's audience that brings workouts, progress and motivation together.

Results

20,000+ downloads in 3 months, a 4.8/5 rating and 37% day-30 retention.

The problem

After a year of development the product still had not reached the quality it needed. The team kept reworking the interfaces, but neither the UX nor the visuals were solving the business goals or meeting the founder's expectations.

UX/UI DESIGN

Programmes and workouts

The core business goal is selling content, which makes workouts one of the most important sections of the app.

There are two kinds: full 10–30 minute sessions with explanations, and short routines — videos from 30 seconds where the trainer quickly shows what to do and how many reps.

The design bar for this section was high, so it took several iterations to reach a result that matched the client's expectations.

Dashboard

The app lets people track their progress through a set of widgets: weight, body measurements, BMI, steps and before/after photo comparison.

People can also log outside activities, such as cycling or walking, directly in the app.

Referral system

To bring in new users and lift engagement we added gamification: I designed an interface where people share the app, see how many friends they have invited and collect rewards.

The rewards themselves got special attention, including 3D badges and animations for the moment a reward is unlocked.

Subscription

The subscription flow was built around ease of use and being as transparent as possible about what people pay for.

Desktop version

The desktop version is not a copy of the mobile app but an experience of its own, made for people who prefer working at a computer.

I reworked the key scenarios for wide screens, kept navigation simple and held the visual language together with the mobile app so the brand reads as one product.

Outcome

The project shipped, and the app collected a lot of positive reviews on the App Store and Google Play.

In the first three months after release it passed 20,000 organic downloads, held an average rating of 4.8 out of 5, and day-30 retention went above 37% — a strong number for a subscription app.

The project gave me experience building a cross-platform product at an early stage, when the product vision and the processes were still taking shape. Beyond designing interfaces, I helped set the visual direction and put the team's design process in place.
