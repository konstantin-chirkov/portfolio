The task

Redesign the campaign creation flow: the path was confusing and clients complained that it was hard to use.

Results

14 → 8 min on the core flow, plus deals with major clients such as Farfetch and Target.

What the research showed

Before starting on the design, I ran a series of meetings with stakeholders. Together we identified the key problems, agreed on priorities and defined the success metrics.

I then ran research with marketers from 5 brands and agencies who launch and run advertising campaigns in Vevel every day, and came away with these insights:

Instead of building a campaign from scratch, marketers cloned the previous one and edited it by hand;

Before launching, many double-checked the budget separately — they were afraid of overspending by accident;

The product had no single control centre: just a list of campaigns, with no overall picture.

UX/UI DESIGN

Campaign dashboard

Instead of a plain campaign list — the whole picture on one screen: KPIs for impressions, clicks, ROAS and budget, a trend chart, and spend broken down by campaign.

Campaign creation wizard

Creating an advertising campaign involved a lot of steps and parameters, so I split the process into clear stages with explicit actions and hints. Users always knew what to do next and how many steps were left.

A catalogue of 27,931 products with search and row-by-row selection. A “show selected only” toggle keeps the focus even on a very long list.

A check before launch

The final step of the wizard became the Review & Launch screen — the entire campaign summarised on one page: budget, products, audience, launch dates. The marketer sees the full picture and can catch a mistake before any money is actually spent — no screenshots, no checking against notes on paper.

After positive feedback from the client and their customers, I was asked to scale the new visual approach across the whole product.

I reviewed the rest of the system, identified the existing patterns and assembled a UI kit with the components and tokens it needed, along with written usage rules. That gave the product a single visual language and made its further development more consistent.

Outcome

Our work ended in a complete interface overhaul, which in turn helped close several contracts with major clients such as Farfetch and Target.

Users rated the updated interface highly: more than 60% reported that it had become easier to use. Time to complete the core flow dropped from 14 to 8 minutes.
